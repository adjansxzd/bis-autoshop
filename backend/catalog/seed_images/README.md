# Product photos for the demo catalog

Put a photo for a product here, named by its **SKU**: `BR-001.jpg`, `OIL-001.png`, `TR-003.webp` …
Then run `python manage.py seed` — photos are attached to products that don't have one yet.

- Formats: JPG, PNG, WebP. Square or 4:3, about 800 px, under 300 KB.
- Use only photos you are allowed to use: your own, or free licences (Unsplash, Pexels).
  Do not copy photos from other shops' websites.
- Products without a photo show a category icon instead.
- A photo can also be uploaded for one product in the admin panel (Products → product → Image).
