# Photo and 3D model credits

## Part photos

Every part has an **illustrative** photo of the same kind of part in `frontend/public/parts/sku/<SKU>.jpg`, taken from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) under free licenses (the product page labels it "Illustrative photo"; brands on a photo may differ from the product). Parts with no honest match (AC-006, CO-003, IG-004, LT-004, SU-003) show their category icon instead. Photos were resized/cropped to 800×600.

To give a part its **own** photo: upload it in the admin panel (Products → photo) or put `<SKU>.jpg` into `backend/catalog/seed_images/` and run `python manage.py seed` — an uploaded photo always wins.
